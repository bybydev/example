ReadAloud requires no account or login.

Core text-to-speech is performed on-device using bundled Core ML resources. Imported document contents are not sent to a remote speech service.

The Free experience can be reviewed without a purchase. ReadAloud Pro uses the StoreKit products configured for the app and unlocks unlimited projects, additional voices, per-chapter narration controls, bulk/offline generation features, and premium themes.

Background audio is used for spoken-document playback. The app supports iPhone only and requires iOS 26 or later.
