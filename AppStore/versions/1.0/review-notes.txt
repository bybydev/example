ReadAloud requires no account or login.

Core text-to-speech is performed on-device using bundled Core ML resources. Imported document contents are not sent to a remote speech service.

The Free experience can be reviewed without a purchase and includes unlimited projects/imports and 3 hours of listening per week. ReadAloud Pro uses the StoreKit products configured for the app and unlocks unlimited listening, offline Generate buttons for preparing chapters/books, additional voices, and premium themes. Per-chapter speeds, queue viewing, pause/resume/cancel, and already queued work remain available without Pro. Free usage-limit and Generate-button triggers show AppFoundation's upsell before the paywall.

Background audio is used for spoken-document playback. The app supports iPhone only and requires iOS 26 or later.
