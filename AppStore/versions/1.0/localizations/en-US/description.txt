Turn EPUBs, PDFs, documents, and pasted text into a private listening library with speech generated directly on your iPhone.

READ DOCUMENTS YOUR WAY
• Import EPUB, PDF, supported documents, or paste text
• Keep chapter and section structure instead of flattening everything into one long block
• Adjust voice and synthesis speed for comfortable listening
• Follow the currently spoken paragraph while reading

LISTEN OFFLINE
• Speech is generated on-device with local Core ML models
• Generate chapters for offline listening
• Reuse finalized audio without synthesizing it again
• Continue from your saved reading and listening position

BUILT FOR LONG-FORM LISTENING
• Organize durable projects in your personal Library
• Continue Listening from where you stopped
• Use background playback, Lock Screen controls, Control Center, and chapter navigation
• Manage generated audio and temporary cache separately

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
ReadAloud is local-first. Your imported documents, normalized text, generated audio, reading progress, and settings stay on your device. No account is required and document text is not sent to a remote speech service.

FREE TO START
Free includes unlimited Library projects, 3 hours of listening each week (including streaming and offline playback), per-chapter narration speeds, prefetch, generate while listening, advanced regeneration, the Heart voice, and ReadAloud Light/Dark themes. Queue viewing, pause, resume, and cancellation remain available for everyone.

READALOUD PRO
Pro unlocks unlimited listening, preparing complete chapters or whole books before listening, the other 27 English Kokoro voices, and additional app themes.

The Free listening allowance resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Usage and the local reset time are shown in your Library. Reading, importing, and managing already queued audio remain available after the listening allowance is used.
