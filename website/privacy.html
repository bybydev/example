Privacy

Your conversations and keys stay under your control.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

BYOK is a bring-your-own-key AI chat client. The app is designed so that your provider credentials and chat data are handled primarily on your device and requests are sent to the AI providers or endpoints you choose.

API keys and credentials

Provider API keys and MCP credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain on your device. They are not included in BYOK backup exports or provider configuration exports.

AI requests

When you use a provider, the content required for your request is sent directly to that provider or endpoint according to the account configuration you selected. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Chats, projects, and files

Conversation history, attachments, and reusable project workspace data such as project instructions, reference files, and project defaults are stored locally by the app. Chat transcripts and provider credentials never leave your device except in requests you send to the providers you configure.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

BYOK keeps local usage and diagnostic information so it can show token, cost, performance, and reliability insights. Diagnostic telemetry is designed to exclude prompts, responses, reasoning content, credentials, attachment contents, tool arguments and results, URLs, and hostnames by default.

Tracking

BYOK does not use advertising tracking and does not sell your personal data.

Backups and exports

Exported backups may contain conversations, attachments, projects, preferences, and non-secret configuration. Provider API keys, MCP credentials, and secret headers are excluded. You are responsible for protecting exported files you create.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as BYOK evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
