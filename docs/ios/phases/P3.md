## Implemented scope

Compact footage preview remains above the scrollable correction/list panels; transport and a simple cue overview/scrubber share the project clock. Keyboard focus reduces preview height. Caption/word selection pauses and seeks; playback highlights/follows its active cue without replacing editing selection or stealing focus.

Per-timed-unit text correction preserves timing and separators, accepts a corrected phrase and hides cleared text without deleting IDs. Manual word/cue timing is explicit and retains native timing/raw recognition. Cue timing scales its contained words; word timing expands the cue as needed. Split before a selected existing word; merge the next cue in the same clip. Fifty-command session undo/redo coalesces consecutive field typing and retains changed-value patches with an 8 MiB estimated budget (one oversized command remains undoable). History is ephemeral; saved edited captions reopen without transcription.

One editor session owns document/playback/selection/history and the sole title/caption save drain. Strict revisions, atomic repository writes, visible retry/reload and newer-draft preservation apply. Stable media composition is independent of captions/panels. CaptionSnapshot and savedDocument() provide the edited input for P4/P5.

No waveform/advanced multi-track editor, search/replace suite, external-display/iPad workspace, styling or export. P4 must replace the plain caption overlay with its shared typography/motion renderer before claiming preview/export parity.
