Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to import, process, and listen to the documents they use with the app.

ReadAloud Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Auto-renewing subscriptions renew according to the terms shown by Apple unless cancelled in App Store subscription settings. Lifetime access is a one-time non-consumable purchase.

The app is provided without a guarantee that every document format, layout, voice, or generated audio result will be error-free. Users should retain their original source documents independently of ReadAloud.
